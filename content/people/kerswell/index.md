## Bio

Dr. Buchanan Kerswell is a PDRA at the University of Liverpool. His research focuses on how reaction kinetics and non-hydrostatic stress affect the major phase changes that define the Mantle Transition Zone (MTZ). This work involves coupling the physics of phase transformations at the grain-scale (following work by Dr. [Xi]({{< ref "/people/xi" >}}), Liverpool) with large numerical simulations of mantle flow using the open-source geodynamics software ASPECT. The resulting catalog of simulated seismic structures are then evaluated and compared against real data---work led by Prof. [Cottaar]({{< ref "/people/cottaar" >}}) and Dr. [Papanagnou]({{< ref "/people/papanagnou" >}}) at Cambridge.
