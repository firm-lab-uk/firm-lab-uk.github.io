## Role

Partner (ASPECT software)

## Bio

Dr. Rene Gassmoeller is a Staff Scientist at GEOMAR and member of the Computational Geodynamics Group. He is a principal developer of the open source geodynamic modeling software [ASPECT](https://aspect.geodynamics.org/) (Advanced Solver for Planetary Evolution, Convection, and Tectonics).
