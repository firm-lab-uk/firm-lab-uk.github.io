## Role

Principal Investigator & Project Lead

## Bio

J. Wheeler is the George Herdman Professor of Geology at the University of Liverpool. His research focuses on the physics of mineral deformation and reaction, and how these processes feed back on large-scale mantle dynamics. He leads the FIRM project, funded by a UKRI NERC Large Grant (NE/V018477/1).
