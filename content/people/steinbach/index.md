Partner (OpenPhase software)

## Bio

Prof. Ingo Steinbach is a Professor and Director of ICAMS at the Ruhr Universitat Bochum. He is the principal developer of the open source phase field simulation software [OpenPhase](https://openphase.rub.de/).
