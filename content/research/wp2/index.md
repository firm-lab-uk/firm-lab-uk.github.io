## Approach

Take the predictions from [WP1]({{< ref "/research/wp1/" >}}) and include them in large-scale numerical geodynamic models of mantle flow.

These numerical geodynamic models will be used to predict what happens when large dense objects (tectonic plates) sink into the mantle (e.g under Japan and South America) and find out what effect the mineral changes have. These models will also be used to simulate what happens when hot less dense material (e.g. under Hawaii and Iceland) rises towards the surface.
