## Approach

Evaluate predictions of mantle mineralogy from [WP1]({{< ref "/research/wp1/" >}}) using (real and synthetic) seismic waves from earthquakes.

These calculations will allow us to test how seismic waves reflect and refract to map the features predicted in [WP2]({{< ref "/research/wp2/" >}}). We will also collect a large data set of observed earthquake waves from across the planet to image the mineral changes occurring deep within and interpret them in terms of on-going flow patterns.
