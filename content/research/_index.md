## Summary

This project aims to transform our understanding of the solid Earth's evolution by exploring the dynamic feedbacks between mineral reactions and mantle convection. While the mantle flows over millions of years, traditional models assume mineral changes are governed purely by depth ('hydrostatic' pressure). However, in a deforming system, localized ('non-hydrostatic') stress can distort the boundary layers where these mineral changes occur---much like wind warping the base of a cloud line. By integrating high-pressure mineral physics experiments, microstructural analysis, multi-scale mathematical modeling, and planetary-scale seismic observations, this research will quantify how flowing mantle dynamics alter mineralogy, and how distorting boundary layers in turn reshape mantle flow.
